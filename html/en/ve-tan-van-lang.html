About us

ViSaViet Travel Services and Trading Joint Stock Company (ViSaViet or ViSaViet Travel for short) was established and began operating in 2003. It was granted its business registration licence by the Ho Chi Minh City Department of Planning & Investment on 14 November 2003.

After many years in the travel industry, we, the founders of ViSaViet, have never stopped looking for the answer to one question: “How can Vietnamese people explore new lands?” We want not only to widen geographical horizons, but also to raise the image of Vietnamese people in the eyes of international friends. We believe that travelling far is not just a journey, but also a safe and trustworthy experience.

With all the passion and dedication of our founding team and staff, and more than 20 years of development, we are proud to be a leading company in Vietnam specialising in quality international tours, affirming long-lasting trust.

ViSaViet offers weekly tour schedules, with more than 20 departures in the peak travel season to make things convenient for customers. Whenever you want to travel, ViSaViet will help you.

ViSaViet is committed to ensuring that all our services comply with the law and keep our customers safe and comfortable.

Over the years, ViSaViet has strengthened close cooperation with domestic and international partners and airlines to monitor quality and improve the company's tour operations.

ViSaViet's growth to where it is today is the result of dedicated service to every customer and of building trust with our partners…

All our staff work to build a friendly and reliable manner so that ViSaViet is always your first choice for every trip. To achieve this, we constantly strive to meet these goals:

  • Become a leading provider of international tours for Vietnamese people
    Customer benefit and satisfaction are the number one priority at ViSaViet
  • Find the best solutions for handling customers' procedures
  • Offer reasonable prices that save customers as much as possible
    Build a professional, creative, effective and lean organisation
  • Become a close friend to our customers and increase the rate of returning customers
    who choose the company
  • Diversify our tours to meet customers' ever-growing needs.

VISION – MISSION

 Vision

ViSaViet Travel strives to become one of the leading, reputable travel companies with a strong brand and a solid position in today's market. The company's goal is to become a global travel brand by 2025.

We are honoured to accompany our customers on their journey to discover and explore new lands. The company strives constantly to become a “familiar face” to customers whenever they want to travel.

Mission

Enriching our customers' lives and improving their quality of life is the foundation of ViSaViet Travel's
core values. These practical values will always drive and guide all  our innovation, creativity and development of travel products and services, with the best quantity and quality of service.

Business management principles

To our customers: Always attentive and dedicated.

To our partners: Cooperation based on respect and long-term commitment, sharing difficulties for mutual benefit.

To our employees: Full benefits and policies, a progressive working environment and opportunities
for growth and promotion.

To our work: Find every way to overcome challenges, be creative, value team
work, and respect creative ideas from managers and staff

We wish all our partners and customers health, happiness and success!
