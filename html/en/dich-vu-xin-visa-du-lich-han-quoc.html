Korea visa documents 2026

Visa applications for most countries follow a fairly standard set of documents, but there are differences depending on each country's criteria and each applicant's situation. For the Korea tourist visa, here are the basic documents: [2]:

Documents for the Korea C-3-9 tourist visa

Documents Details
Personal documents
  1. Passport: the new original and the old one (if any)
  2. ID card / citizen ID (original) for the Consulate to verify the information
  3. 3.5×4.5 cm photo (white background). See detailed instructions here
  4. Visa application form (with photo attached). For how to fill it in, see the article: Korea visa application form
  5. Travel itinerary (translated into English or Korean, no notarisation needed)
  6. Marriage certificate (if any)
  7. Children's birth certificates (if travelling with you)
  8. Hotel booking and round-trip flight confirmation
Proof of employment For employees: 

  • Labour contract or employment verification letter (adding social insurance records strengthens the application)
  • Leave application

For private business owners: 

  • Business registration licence
  • Tax payment certificates for the last 3 months

For students:

  • Student card or student status certificate.
  • School leave request

If the visa applicant is a school student or a minor under 18:

  • Parents' consent to travel alone (must be certified by the local authority)
  • Applicant's birth certificate, parents' marriage certificate and parents' citizen ID cards (with notarised translations)

If the visa applicant is a university student over 18 who depends financially on their parents:

  • Applicant's birth certificate
  • Parents' marriage certificate
  • Parents' citizen ID cards (with notarised translations)

For retirees: 

  • Pension payment confirmation
  • Retirement certificate
  • Social insurance book

For freelancers: A certified résumé clearly stating your occupation

Proof of finances For employees: 

  • Payroll records
  • Bank account statement (last 03 months)
  • Bank deposit balance confirmation (issued within the last 02 weeks) totalling more than US$5,000 (about VND 110,000,000 or more), with a term of over 01 month, plus the original savings book.

If the visa applicant is a school student, a minor under 18, or over 18 but a student who depends financially on their parents:

  • Financial sponsorship letter
  • Parents' proof of finances: a savings book with a term of over 1 month of at least VND 110,000,000; balance confirmation; statements
  • Parents' employment documents and land use right certificate

For freelancers: 

  • Proof of income
  • You may also submit a land use right certificate, car registration, property sale contract, credit card limit confirmation, house rental contract,…

Documents for the 5-year Korea visa C-3-91

The documents for the Korea C-3-91 visa are quite similar to the requirements for the C-3-9 visa, but you also need to provide:

  1. Certificate of residence information (form CT07 issued by the ward or commune police): original and notarised translation.
  2. Proof of property ownership in 1 of the 3 major cities (Hanoi, HCMC, Da Nang)

Documents for the Korea family visit visa C-3-1

►Documents the inviter needs to prepare

  • A signed family visit invitation letter clearly stating the reason
  • Family relationship certificate
  • Inviter's ID card / passport
  • Inviter's proof of finances

►Documents the invitee needs to prepare

  1. Korea family visit visa application form with a 3.5x 4.5cm photo attached
  2. Copy of citizen ID / ID card
  3. Original passport with a copy
  4. Form CT07 confirming residence information

Documents for the Korea trade/business visa

Documents from the inviter in Korea

  • A stamped invitation letter stating the invitation period, the reason, and details of the inviter and invitee
  • A stamped guarantee letter
  • Certificate of seal impression
  • Copy of the business registration certificate
  • Tax payment certificate for the last 3 months
  • Detailed itinerary and description of the work in Korea
  • Documents proving the business purpose, such as contracts, import-export declarations, cooperation proposal letters,…

Documents from the invitee

  • Original passport with a copy, valid for at least 6 months
  • Visa application form with a 3.5x 4.5cm photo attached
  • Proof of occupation documents
  • Proof of occupation

► Employees:

  • Scanned labour contract
  • Decision assigning the business trip to Korea
  • Social insurance: screenshots of the VssID app showing the health insurance card and the full social insurance contribution history

► Business owners:

  • Scanned business registration licence
  • Scanned receipts of taxes paid in the last 3 months
  • Copy of citizen ID card
  • Documents proving the business purpose, such as contracts, import-export declarations, cooperation proposal letters,…

A Korea visa for an online teacher

It was peak cherry blossom season in Korea, and this is a question many ViSaViet customers have worried about because proving income and employment is complicated. In reality, if the application is well prepared, the chance of getting a Korea visa is still very high, as our customer found!

So how can an online teacher prove their teaching work in this case?

  • Provide photos and videos of lessons.
  • Messages exchanged with parents or through teaching platforms.
  • Details of the social media/Facebook/Zalo accounts used to connect with students.
  • Account statements showing income from the work 

► Beyond these points, ViSaViet's consultants also look at other parts of the customer's profile that can improve the approval rate, such as travel history and personal records. Transparent, clear information makes the Korea visa review go more smoothly.

Online teachers can still easily apply for a Korea visa if they know how to prepare suitable documents, just like our customer!

The customer owns a business in Vietnam in trade and import-export. After receiving an invitation from a Korean partner to attend an international trade fair, he decided to apply for a business visa so he could meet partners and expand his market.

ViSaViet noted that the customer had never been to Korea, so the purpose of the trip and the trade fair dates needed to be clearly proven. A reasonable explanation was also needed of the purpose, finances, tax payments as required and business activities, so that the application would be convincing enough for the Korea Visa Application Center.

► Thanks to ViSaViet's careful preparation and well-optimised application strategy, the customer received a Korea business visa after just 12 working days, right on schedule for the trade fair! 

A 10-year Korea visa for a Vietnamese doctor

Not everyone can get the 10-year Korea visa! It is only for certain special groups, including doctors, lawyers, professors, directors of large businesses…

This customer is a general internal medicine specialist working at a large private hospital in HCMC, with more than 15 years of experience.

With a rich travel history, the customer had entered Korea 4 times and had a strong record of travel to developed countries such as the US, Japan, Australia and Europe. The customer owns property in HCMC and has savings and a fairly stable monthly income.

Most importantly, the medical practising licence was a key factor in getting the 10-year visa approved.

► ViSaViet advised the customer step by step in preparing a proper application, and the customer was granted a 10-year Korea visa after just 8 working days! With this visa, each entry allows a stay of up to 90 days, ideal for business trips, holidays or medical conferences in Korea.

Korea visa Q&A with ViSaViet

Can I change information after submitting the application?

Once the application is submitted, all the information has been received and can no longer be changed. Be sure to check it carefully before submitting to limit mistakes.

Do you help translate documents?

Yes, the company will help translate the documents the Consulate requires so that your visa application is as complete as possible.

Does ViSaViet help book flights?

Yes, ViSaViet helps customers who wish to book flights after the visa procedure is completed or the visa result is issued.

Do I need to submit original documents, or are notarised copies enough?

For a Korea visa application, you must provide your original passport. All other documents can be originals or certified copies no more than 3 months old.

Do I need to book an appointment before submitting documents at your office?

No, you can bring your documents to the office during working hours.

After submitting, how can I follow the progress of my application?

A ViSaViet support specialist will follow your application and update you on its progress, so you are always informed promptly.

Our service is available to customers in every province and city of Vietnam, not just Hanoi and HCMC. Just contact us online or by phone and we will help you quickly and effectively.
