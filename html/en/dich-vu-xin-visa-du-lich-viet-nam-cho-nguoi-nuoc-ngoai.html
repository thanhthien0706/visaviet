What is a Vietnam tourist visa for foreigners

Tourist visa (DL visa): one of the most common types of visa in Vietnam. It is issued to foreign citizens for tourism and does not apply to foreigners who intend to work or do business during their stay in Vietnam.

Foreigners who travel to Vietnam on an electronic visa (e-visa) can stay for up to 90 days. It is even more convenient for those who want to visit several other countries and then return to Vietnam while the e-visa is still valid, as they do not need to apply for a new visa.

Conditions for foreigners to get a Vietnam tourist entry visa

The conditions depend on the type of Vietnam visa the foreigner applies for, and are set out as follows:

✅Conditions for an electronic visa (e-visa)

  • The foreigner's passport is still valid and the holder does not fall under the cases of suspended entry set out in Article 21 of the Law on Entry, Exit, Transit and Residence of Foreigners in Vietnam [2]Reference link: https://bocongan.gov.vn/pbgdpl/van-ban-quy-pham.html?ItemID=2699.
  • The foreigner is a citizen of a country or territory on the list eligible for e-visas under the Government's Resolution.

✅Conditions for a tourist entry approval letter

  • The sponsoring company is an international tour operator in Vietnam with the legal status to sponsor foreigners entering Vietnam for tourism.
  • Foreigners, and Vietnamese entering on foreign passports, are not permitted to enter Vietnam if they fall under the cases of “suspended entry” under Vietnamese law.

Documents for a Vietnam tourist visa for foreigners

To apply for a Vietnam tourist visa, foreigners need to prepare the following documents for each type:

Documents for a Vietnam electronic visa (e-visa)

  1. A passport valid for more than 6 months
  2. A 4×6 portrait photo of the foreigner with the face clearly visible.

Documents for an entry approval letter

  1. A passport still valid as required by Vietnamese entry rules (scanned copy).
  2. Application form for an entry approval letter for foreigners (form NA2). Download it and see detailed instructions here
  3. A company with the required legal status needs the sponsoring company's business registration certificate or investment certificate (notarised copy)
  4. A notarised copy of the sponsoring company's tax registration certificate.
  5. A letter registering the company seal and the signature of the legal representative who signs the application (form NA16). Download it and see detailed instructions here
  6. The sponsoring company's digital signature USB (token), as required by the law on electronic transactions.
  7. A letter of introduction for the person submitting the application, or a power of attorney for the submitting employee, issued by the sponsoring company.
  8. ID card / citizen ID of the representative submitting the application.

After preparing the documents required by the competent authority, the sponsoring company's representative in Vietnam submits the application online on the National Public Service Portal of the Ministry of Public Security [3]Reference link: https://dichvucong.bocongan.gov.vn/?home=1

Vietnam tourist visa procedure for foreigners

Electronic visa (e-visa)

✅ Step 1: Go to the Immigration Department's online portal and enter the information required for the electronic visa (e-visa) application:

Link: https://evisa.xuatnhapcanh.gov.vn/

  • Upload a photo of the passport data page
  • Upload the portrait photo file you prepared earlier.
  • Fill in the required information on the form completely and accurately.

After entering all the required information, check it very carefully to make sure there are no mistakes.

✅ Step 2:Pay the e-visa fee by e-wallet or bank account.

✅ Step 3: Check the result of your e-visa application.

Use the electronic application code to check the result. If approved, download and print it to enter Vietnam.

* Note: An e-visa application will not be accepted if documents are missing or incorrect, or the information cannot be verified.

Tourist entry approval letter

A Vietnam entry approval letter is a document of approval from the immigration authority that allows foreigners to enter Vietnam for purposes such as tourism, business, investment, work or visiting relatives.

An entry approval letter is a document issued by the competent Vietnamese authority to foreigners who want to apply for a visa and enter Vietnam. After receiving the entry approval letter, the foreigner presents it when having the Vietnam entry visa affixed at a Vietnamese Embassy abroad, or at a Vietnamese international airport or border gate.

Entry approval letters are issued by the Immigration Department, Vietnam Ministry of Public Security. The agency, organisation or individual inviting a foreigner to Vietnam needs to prepare the application for an entry approval letter and submit it to the Immigration Department.

The steps to obtain an entry approval letter for a foreigner are as follows: 

✅ Step 1: An international tour operator in Vietnam completes the sponsorship procedure for the foreigner to visit Vietnam as a tourist. It is reviewed by the immigration authority.

✅ Step 2: Receive the entry approval letter

Once the entry approval letter is issued, the sponsoring company sends it to the foreigner, who uses it to collect the Vietnam tourist visa according to the previously registered details.

✅ Step 3: Collect the Vietnam visa

Foreigners who registered to collect the visa at an Embassy bring their passport to the Vietnamese Embassy. Those who registered at a port of entry receive the visa during immigration clearance.

Vietnam tourist visa fees

✅For the entry approval letter

  • Single-entry visa: US$25 for 1 month or 3 months
  • Multiple-entry visa: US$50 for 1 month or 3 months
  • Multiple-entry visa: US$135 for 1 year

✅For the electronic visa

  • US$25 for a single-entry visa
  • US$50 for a multiple-entry visa
